## Purpose

The current C8 U(1) route has a potentially nonempty *classical* intersection between:

- one published exact-GR PPN family;
- the fully nonlinear classical no-unwanted-gravity-scalar surface of the nonprojectable U(1) theory;
- the direct RTK coefficient slice `beta0=2`.

This note freezes the next interpretation gate before treating that classical intersection as a viable completion.

## Literature input

Hamiltonian analysis: Shinji Mukohyama, Ryo Namba, Rio Saitou, Yota Watanabe, `arXiv:1504.07357`, *Hamiltonian analysis of nonprojectable Hořava-Lifshitz gravity with U(1) symmetry*.

Its abstract-level theorem is sufficient for the present gate:

1. the scalar graviton is absent at the fully nonlinear **classical** level iff two specific couplings are exactly zero;
2. the corresponding operators are marginal for any Lifshitz dynamical exponent;
3. those operators should therefore be generated by quantum corrections even if absent in the bare action;
4. the generic theory thus contains the scalar graviton.

In the action dictionary used in the RTK U(1) appendix, these are the coefficients of

`a_i a^i sigma`

and

`D_i a^i sigma`,

called `(eta1,eta2)` in the Hamiltonian paper and `(sigma1,sigma2)` in the PPN action after the conventional `eta0=1`, `Omega=Lambda_g` normalization.

## RTK candidate surface

The classical candidate currently under test is

`a1=1`,

`kappa=1`,

`sigma1=sigma2=0`,

`beta0=2`,

`gamma1=-1`.

The zero values of `sigma1,sigma2` are not optional cosmetic choices: under the published Hamiltonian theorem they are precisely the special surface required to remove the unwanted gravitational scalar nonlinearly in that gravity sector.

## Technical-naturalness rule

A classical parameter surface is not promoted to a final C8/C9 completion merely because the tree-level equations admit it.

For the current U(1) route, acceptance requires at least one of the following to be derived from the **same completed action**:

1. an additional exact symmetry/Ward identity that forbids both `a_i a^i sigma` and `D_i a^i sigma` operators;
2. a structural degeneracy/constraint identity that keeps the unwanted scalar absent even after the corresponding radiatively allowed counterterms are included;
3. a demonstrated RG fixed surface on which the two beta functions vanish consistently;
4. a quantitatively high cutoff plus controlled tuning analysis showing that induced coefficients remain below the physical tolerance over the entire validity range.

Absent one of these mechanisms, `sigma1=sigma2=0` is classified as **classically admissible but not technically natural**.

## Important scope

This is **not** a quantum no-go theorem for all U(1), Hořava, DHOST, auxiliary-field, or RTK completions.

It does not prove that no extra protecting symmetry exists. It says only that the already-used U(1) symmetry by itself does not justify ignoring operators that the Hamiltonian literature identifies as allowed marginal terms expected from quantum corrections.

It also does not replace an explicit one-loop/RG calculation for the final RTK action.
