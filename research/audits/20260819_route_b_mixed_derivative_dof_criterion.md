# Route-B mixed-derivative DOF design criterion — 2026-08-19

## Why this criterion exists

The implemented RTK/Khronon scalar response requires a momentum-dependent kinetic operator at quadratic order,

`K(q) = K [1 + q^2/M^2]`,

which is naturally generated by preferred-foliation mixed-derivative structures such as spatial derivatives of the extrinsic curvature. The companion audit `20260819_route_b_khronometric_dispersion_nongo.md` proves that a standard khronometric/Hořava completion that modifies only the spatial potential cannot reproduce the RTK rational target exactly.

However, admitting mixed spatial/time derivative operators is not sufficient: the completion must also preserve the intended physical degree-of-freedom count.

## Primary-literature warning

The following primary works are directly relevant:

- M. Colombo, A. E. Gümrükçüoğlu, T. P. Sotiriou, *Hořava gravity with mixed derivative terms*, arXiv:1410.6360 / Phys. Rev. D 91, 044021 (2015). This work studies terms containing spatial derivatives of the extrinsic curvature, including structures schematically of the form `(D_i K_jk)^2`, and shows that mixed derivatives modify propagators/dispersion relations.
- A. Coates, M. Colombo, A. E. Gümrükçüoğlu, T. P. Sotiriou, *The uninvited guest in mixed derivative Hořava Gravity*, arXiv:1604.04215 / Phys. Rev. D 94, 084014 (2016). For the minimal non-projectable theory including all mixed operators allowed by the adopted rules, the propagating content becomes four degrees of freedom rather than the usual three; the additional mode is scalar and is unstable at low energies.
- J. Klusoň, *Hamiltonian Analysis of Mixed Derivative Hořava-Lifshitz Gravity*, arXiv:1607.08361. The canonical analysis independently confirms one additional scalar degree of freedom relative to non-projectable Hořava gravity for that mixed-derivative theory.

Later discussion of Lorentz-violation naturalness also emphasizes that radiative closure of the non-projectable mixed-derivative theory can generate terms quadratic in time derivatives of the lapse, making the extra-scalar issue unavoidable unless protected by additional structure.

## Important scope distinction

The literature warning is **not** a proof that the isolated operator `(D_i K_jk)^2` by itself necessarily creates the extra scalar in every restricted EFT. The stronger 2016 result concerns the mixed-derivative theory when the operator content required by the chosen symmetry/power-counting prescription is included. In particular, the dangerous enlarged kinetic sector can involve time derivatives of quantities related to the lapse.

Therefore the correct conclusion is not “mixed derivatives are impossible”. It is:

> A Route-B completion may use mixed derivatives only if its constraint/degeneracy structure is explicitly demonstrated to retain the intended scalar content and if that restriction is stable under the declared EFT/radiative closure.

## RTK Route-B acceptance criterion

Any explicit coupled completion proposed to close C7 must now satisfy all of the following before it is considered admissible:

1. **Quadratic target:** reproduce `omega^2 = c_a^2 q^2/(1+q^2/M^2)` at the RTK scalar level.
2. **Kinetic origin:** explicitly identify the operator or auxiliary reduction generating the `q^2` kinetic denominator; a potential-only polynomial is insufficient.
3. **No unintended extra scalar:** perform Hamiltonian/constraint or equivalent degeneracy analysis proving that the completion carries no additional propagating scalar beyond the intended Khronon scalar (and the causal RT localization variables are not miscounted as free local particles).
4. **Lapse/shift control:** show whether the proposed mixed structure gives time derivatives to lapse/shift or otherwise changes their constraint role. If it does, demonstrate the degeneracy/secondary constraint that removes the would-be extra mode.
5. **Radiative scope:** state whether the restricted operator subset is technically stable. If loops/symmetry require the dangerous omitted mixed operators, C9 remains open even if the tree-level DOF count passes.
6. **Cosmological reduction:** after solving nondynamical constraints, recover the already frozen `K,G,M` quadratic target and the established long-wave thermodynamics.

## Candidate frameworks suggested by the literature

Spatially covariant gravity provides a broader Hamiltonian language in which the lapse, extrinsic curvature, intrinsic curvature and spatial derivatives can be treated directly. General Hamiltonian results show that spatially covariant theories can be organized to propagate a controlled number of degrees of freedom, and degeneracy conditions can remove unwanted scalar modes in suitable subclasses.

This makes a **degenerate spatially covariant completion** or a constrained auxiliary-field realization a better Route-B target than unrestricted mixed-derivative Hořava gravity.
