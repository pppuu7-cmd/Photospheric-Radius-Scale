`omega^2 = (b1 x + b2 x^2 + b3 x^3)/A0`.

`(b1 x+b2 x^2+b3 x^3)(1+x) = A0 c x`.

Coefficient matching forces the highest coefficient to zero recursively and finally gives

`b1=b2=b3=c=0`.

Therefore there is **no nontrivial exact match** between a constant-kinetic finite polynomial potential and the implemented RTK rational dispersion.
