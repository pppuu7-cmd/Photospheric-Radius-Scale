## Scientific interpretation

This probe resolves an ambiguity in the matched-center signature atlas:

1. Over k <= 0.5 h/Mpc, the intrinsic RTK/LCDM matter-power residual is nearly scale-flat because this range is mostly below k_star and c_a^2 is extremely small.
2. The strong ~4% matched low-k tilt previously seen is therefore mostly generated by correlated parameter retuning, not by direct crossing of the Khronon transition within that k-range.
3. The actual implemented scale-dependent turnover becomes visible at z~0 only for k of order 1--3 h/Mpc and shifts rapidly to much higher k with redshift.
