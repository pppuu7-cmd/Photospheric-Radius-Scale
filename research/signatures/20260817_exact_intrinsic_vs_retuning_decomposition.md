## Scientific consequence

The best degeneracy-breaking test should not rely on the matched low-k P(k) tilt alone. More robust intrinsic signatures currently are:

1. late-time fσ8/growth shift (~2% before retuning, still ~1.5--1.9% after retuning),
2. a roughly scale-flat late-time linear-power amplitude shift (~1--2.4% at fixed shared parameters),
3. percent-level late-time geometry differences at fixed shared parameters,
4. CMB polarization structure that can be strongly hidden by correlated standard-parameter shifts.

A joint CMB-polarization + geometry + scale-resolved growth analysis is therefore a more appropriate falsification direction than a one-observable shape claim.
