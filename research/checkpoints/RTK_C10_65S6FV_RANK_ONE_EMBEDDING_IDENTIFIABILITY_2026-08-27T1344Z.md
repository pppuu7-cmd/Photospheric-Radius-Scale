# RTK C10.65s6fV rank-one embedding identifiability theorem

Parent C10.65s6fU proved that a nonempty local projectable two-scalar rank-one kinematic class exists and carries at most one finite pole. C10.65s6fV asks whether that information is sufficient to select one full fixed action.

For `K=kappa v v^T`, the matrix determinant lemma gives `det(M-zK)=det(M)[1-z kappa v^T M^{-1}v]`; hence the finite pole is independent of the external/source vector. The exact response `s^T(M-zK)^{-1}s` nevertheless depends on `s`, so source direction is not fixed by pole location.

A continuous same-pole potential family also exists. For `v=(1,1)`, `kappa=1`, take `M(t)=1/2[[2+t,2-t],[2-t,2+t]]`, `t>0`. Then `det M=2t`, `v^T M^{-1}v=1`, and `det(M-zvv^T)=2t(1-z)` for every positive `t`, while static/source responses vary with source choice.

Classification: `C10_65S6FV_RANK_ONE_FULL_EMBEDDING_NON_IDENTIFIABLE_PASS_SCOPED`. This is not a no-go. It proves that rank-one plus one-pole data cannot uniquely source-lock the potential matrix or geometric source coupling.
