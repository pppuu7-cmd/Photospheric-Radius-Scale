`L_S=sigma[sigma1 a_i a^i+sigma2 D_i a^i+2Lambda_g-R]`.

`(2Omega-eta0 R+eta1 a_i a^i+eta2 D_i a^i)sigma`.

After the conventional normalization `eta0=1` and `Omega=Lambda_g`, coefficient matching of the same invariant basis gives

`eta1=sigma1`, `eta2=sigma2`.
